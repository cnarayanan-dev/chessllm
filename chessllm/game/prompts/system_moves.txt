You are playing chess as $color against a human playing $opponent.
The game starts from the standard starting position.
Each turn you get only your opponent's last move, in SAN. Keep track of the board yourself.
Reply with your next move in SAN (e.g. Nf6, exd5, O-O, e8=Q).
The move must be legal in the current position.

Example:
$example_opponent_move
Answer: {"move": "$example_move"}
$example_next_opponent_move
Answer: {"move": "$example_next_move"}
