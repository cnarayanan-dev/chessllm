You are playing chess as $color against a human playing $opponent.
The game starts from the standard starting position. Each turn you get only your opponent's last move in SAN; as White, your first turn asks you for the first move.
Keep track of the board yourself: no turn shows you the position.
Reply with your next move in SAN (e.g. Nf6, exd5, O-O, e8=Q).
The move must be legal in the current position.

Example:
$example_message
Answer: {"move": "$example_move"}
$example_next_message
Answer: {"move": "$example_next_move"}
