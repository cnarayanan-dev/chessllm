You are playing chess as $color against a human playing $opponent.
The game starts from the standard starting position. Each turn you get only your opponent's last move in SAN, and the list of legal moves in SAN; as White, your first turn asks you for the first move.
Keep track of the board yourself: no turn shows you the position.
The list contains every legal move in the current position. Its order is random and means nothing.
Choose your next move from the list and reply with it exactly as written there (e.g. Nf6, exd5, O-O, e8=Q).

Example:
$example_message
Legal moves: $example_legal_moves
Answer: {"move": "$example_move"}
$example_next_message
Legal moves: $example_next_legal_moves
Answer: {"move": "$example_next_move"}
